What it does

A living picture of your wiki

Built for LLM wikis (sources, entities, concepts, syntheses, an index and a log) and useful on any vault.

A brain made of your notes

Linked pages share a region of the brain. A soft cloud shows its volume and the neurons fill it, from ten pages to ten thousand.

Flow: information reaches every page

A wave leaves the index and crosses the links hop by hop, with rings, comets and glowing links, and a counter of the pages reached.

Replay the wiki growing

Watch the pages return in creation order, with what the log recorded each day, a scrubber, speed and loop.

Search and Ask

Words, tag:, links:>3, edited:<7d, is:orphan, regex. Press Enter to ask: the answer spreads along the links.

Filters and saved views

Type, folder, tag, links, dates, direction, a filter query and the log. Active filters are chips you remove with one click.

Select, isolate, export

Select a group by click, area or lobe; isolate it, copy it as links, turn it into an Obsidian Canvas, or save the view as a PNG.

Lint what is broken

Orphans, broken links and untyped pages get a ring and a clickable list, so the wiki stays healthy.

Fast and private

Canvas 2D, no network, no telemetry. About 25 ms a frame at 10,000 pages in our synthetic benchmark; full neurons only where they are big enough to see.

Four languages

English, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish and French, following Obsidian's language.

How it works

From a vault to a brain

Everything is derived from what Obsidian already knows.

  1. Your vaultNotes, [[links]], tags, frontmatter, wiki/log.md
  2. A directed graphA link leaves the page that holds it and lands on the page it points to
  3. A neuron modelPages add up inputs, fire at a threshold, learn and fade
  4. The brainLobes, dendrites, axons and a wave you can watch

The analogy

The neuron model

The picture follows the biology, so what you see says something about your wiki.

BiologyIn the plugin
DendritesInputs: the pages that link to this one
AxonOutputs: the links this page contains
Synaptic strengthHow often a link is repeated; links used together grow stronger and fade when idle
Firing thresholdA page fires all-or-nothing when the charge it received reaches a threshold that grows with its inputs; the arc around it shows how close it is
InhibitionA contradicts: link in the frontmatter lowers the target's charge; its spikes are red
MyelinLinks between busy pages conduct faster, in jumps
AttentionPages that are similar but not linked light up for a moment, with weights

Install

Get LLM Wiki Brain

The plugin is not in the Obsidian community directory yet. Install it with BRAT or by hand.

  1. BRAT (beta): in Obsidian, install the BRAT plugin, run BRAT: Add a beta plugin and enter jonyfs/llm-wiki-brain.
  2. By hand: download main.js, manifest.json and styles.css from the latest release into <vault>/.obsidian/plugins/llm-wiki-brain/.
  3. Enable LLM Wiki Brain in Settings → Community plugins, then click the brain icon in the ribbon.

FAQ

Questions

Does it send my notes anywhere?

No. The plugin makes no network requests and has no telemetry. The demo on this page uses made-up pages.

Does it work on any vault?

Yes. By default it reads the wiki folder; empty that setting to use the whole vault. A page's type comes from the frontmatter type, then from folder names.

How many pages can it show?

From a handful to 10,000 (the limit is a setting; the default is 800). A frame takes about 25 ms at 10,000 pages in our synthetic benchmark; your results depend on your machine.

Does it work on mobile?

It is not desktop-only and handles touch (drag, pinch), but it has been tested mostly on desktop.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish and French. It follows Obsidian's language, and you can pick another in the settings.

Is it related to the LLM Wiki Template?

Yes, same author. The LLM Wiki Template is a starting point for a wiki an AI assistant maintains; this plugin shows that wiki as a brain. This site reuses the template's design system.
